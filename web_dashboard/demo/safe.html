Easy banana bread

Fictional example written for testing

Mash three ripe bananas in a bowl. Stir in melted butter, a cup of sugar, one egg and a teaspoon of vanilla.

Add a teaspoon of baking soda, a pinch of salt and one and a half cups of flour. Pour into a greased loaf pan and bake at 175 °C for about an hour, until a toothpick comes out clean.

Let it cool for ten minutes before slicing. It keeps for three days wrapped on the counter.
